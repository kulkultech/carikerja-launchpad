Independent builders succeed by solving specific problems for specific people. If you offer "general programming" to everyone, you appeal to no one. This step guides you in narrowing down your services and target clients.

---

## 🎯 Step Objectives
*   Define your primary technical **Service Offering**.
*   Select your target business **Niche** (Industry/Client Category).
*   Document these choices in your upcoming profile setup.

---

## 🏃‍♂️ Action Guide

### 1. Define Your Technical Service Offering
Focus on packaging your skills into concrete, outcome-based deliverables. Choose one primary offering to start:
*   **SEO Landing Pages:** Fast, high-conversion marketing pages built with Astro or React for businesses that need to capture online leads.
*   **Automated Scheduling Systems:** Integrating booking flows (e.g. Calendly, custom schedulers) and instant notification triggers (SMS/Email/WhatsApp) to remove administrative overhead.
*   **Database/CRM Sync:** Linking contact forms directly into client spreadsheets, Notion databases, or CRMs to automate manual lead entry.

### 2. Choose Your Niche (Target Market)
Identify local service businesses or local startups where your service makes immediate sense. Some high-value niches include:
*   **Health & Wellness:** Dental practices, veterinary clinics, physiotherapy centers, local gyms.
*   **Home Services:** Pest control, plumbing contractors, home renovations, landscape architects.
*   **B2C Local:** Specialty bakeries, boutique coffee rosters, independent real estate agencies.

### 3. Niche Fit Formulation
Construct your value proposition using this simple formula:
> *"I help [target niche] automate [business pain point] by building [technical service offering] so they can increase [business outcome]."*

*Example: "I help boutique gyms automate member check-ins and session bookings by building custom React dashboards so they can decrease manual front-desk administration."*
