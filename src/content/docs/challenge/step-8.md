Once you have a targeted prospect list, you must reach out. Good outreach is short, acknowledges the client's current setup, offers a specific improvement, and makes replying effortless. In this step, you draft your pitch templates.

---

## 🎯 Step Objectives
*   Understand the anatomy of a high-converting cold pitch.
*   Draft personalized pitch templates using AI companion copy-writing.
*   Prepare your outreach schedule.

---

## 🏃‍♂️ Action Guide

### 1. Anatomy of a Cold Pitch
Your outreach message should have 4 simple parts:
1.  **Personalized Hook:** Mention their business name and something positive about their work.
2.  **The Gaps Observation:** Politely note the technical bottleneck you observed. E.g., *"I noticed booking a session currently redirects to an email draft..."*
3.  **The Solution Offer:** Briefly describe how you can fix it. E.g., *"I automate this by building simple inline booking widgets..."*
4.  **No-Pressure Call to Action (CTA):** Keep it low friction. Do not ask for a sale; ask for a quick chat. E.g., *"Do you have 10 minutes next Tuesday for a quick chat? If not, no worries."*

### 2. Draft Your Pitch Templates
Write 2 outreach templates tailored to your 5 target leads. One should be a short LinkedIn/Instagram message (under 100 words), and the other an email template (under 150 words).

*Example Prompt to refine your pitch:*
```text
Context: I am pitching a custom React scheduler to a veterinary clinic named Acme Vets. 
Their current website requires people to call during office hours to schedule visits.

Task: Write a personalized 120-word email pitch. Focus on how a booking system saves their admin time and secures after-hours bookings. Keep the tone helpful, professional, and completely non-pushy.
```
Save these drafts. You will use them to launch your outreach campaign.
