Welcome to the **Carikerja Launchpad Onboarding Challenge**. To join the program, you must complete the following 10 steps, which are designed to transition you into an AI-assisted engineering and independent freelance workflow.

This challenge is a practical screening process. We focus on your **code understanding**, **prompting strategy**, and **collaboration etiquette** rather than manual syntax memorization.
