Welcome to the **Carikerja Launchpad Community**! 

This page serves as a directory of all the developers who have successfully completed the onboarding challenge and launched their independence path.

---

## 👥 Meet Our Launchers

Our launchers are independent consultants, freelancers, and agency owners who use modern AI tools to build high-quality software solutions and solve real-world business problems.

Look at the sidebar under **Our Launchers** to view individual profiles!

---

## ✍️ How to Add Yourself

As part of the [10-Step Challenge](/challenge/), you are required to add your profile here.

1. Create a new file in `src/content/docs/launchers/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME.md`.
2. Add your name, bio, service offerings, and target niche.
3. Commit (without GPG signing!) and open a Pull Request.

Once your PR is merged, your profile will automatically appear in the sidebar navigation!
