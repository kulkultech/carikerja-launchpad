# Carikerja Design Principles

This repository follows the design principles and specifications outlined in the GetDesign framework.

Learn more at: **[getdesign.md](https://getdesign.md)**

---

## 🎨 Aesthetic Intent
*   **Vibe:** Premium, professional, wealth-and-growth growth teals/emeralds, dark-first, and highly engaging.
*   **Visual Elements:** Glassmorphism, tailored HSL color palettes, responsive cards, clean typography, and interactive calculators.
*   **Aims:** Make independent technical freelancing look like a premium, lucrative, and structured business path.
